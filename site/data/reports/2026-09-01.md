# US Robotics Intelligence Brief — 2026-09-01

> **Coverage:** United States humanoid robotics first, with adjacent embodied-AI companies where they change the talent, capital, or technical landscape. Monetary values are dated observations—not live appraisals.

## Executive signal

The US ecosystem is splitting into three coupled layers:

1. **Integrated humanoid builders** — Figure, Apptronik, Agility, Boston Dynamics, Tesla/Optimus, 1X's US operation, Persona, and Foundation.
2. **Cross-embodiment intelligence companies** — Skild AI, Physical Intelligence, and Generalist AI.
3. **Production physical-AI comparators** — companies such as Dexterity that generate evidence about reliability, integration, and industrial economics without requiring humanoid morphology.

The Bay Area dominates frontier-model capital and startup density. Austin is becoming unusually important for humanoid hardware, production, and industrial deployment. Boston and Pittsburgh remain deeper institutional robotics clusters than startup counts alone imply. Oregon and Houston matter because they connect humanoids to factories, logistics, shipyards, and hazardous work.

## Recommended project move

Do **not** start a disconnected new portfolio project every morning. Use repeated evidence across several companies to deepen one coherent embodied-AI system. The best immediate response to the current roles is a compact **real-time robot-control and learning stack**:

1. C++/ROS 2 control loop with measured timing and watchdog behavior;
2. estimator and controller tested under noise, delay, saturation, and model mismatch;
3. Python experiment pipeline with deterministic simulation and ablations;
4. perception or learned-policy component whose task-level failures are measured;
5. concise engineering report showing update rate, latency, stability margins, and deployment assumptions.

This aligns more strongly with the current market than a polished simulation video without systems evidence.
