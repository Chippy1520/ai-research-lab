SmolVLA + LeRobot

Vision-language-action · arXiv 2506.01844 · June 2025

A 450M-parameter VLA trained on community LeRobot data. Flow matching, layer skipping, 64 visual tokens, and an asynchronous inference stack that decouples chunk prediction from execution. The guide covers SmolVLM-2, the action expert, community-data hygiene, and how to read the real-world vs simulation numbers.

VGGT: Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer

3D vision · CVPR 2025 Best Paper · arXiv 2503.11651

One feed-forward transformer that, from 1–hundreds of images, predicts cameras, depth, point maps, and tracks in under a second. Alternating frame/global attention, over-complete training, and why combining depth + cameras at inference beats the dedicated point-map head.

ACT: Action Chunking with Transformers

Imitation learning · RSS 2023 · arXiv 2304.13705

The paper that made chunked imitation the default. Compounding error as a horizon problem, CVAE style latents, temporal ensembling, ALOHA hardware, and why ACT is still the first policy you train in LeRobot.

STLight

Spatio-temporal prediction · arXiv 2411.10198 · November 2024

A fully convolutional replacement for ConvLSTM / PredRNN. Joint space–time patches, STLMixer (local + dilated), pixel-shuffle decode. SOTA on Moving MNIST / TaxiBJ / KTH with a fraction of the FLOPs — the efficiency paper that actually measures FLOPs.
