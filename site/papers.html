SmolVLA + LeRobot

Vision-language-action · arXiv 2506.01844 · June 2025

Case study: teaching an intern to put a cube in a bowl on a cheap arm — look, plan a short phrase of motion, keep moving while thinking ahead. Then the same steps as VLM, chunk, async stack.

VGGT: Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer

3D vision · CVPR 2025 Best Paper · arXiv 2503.11651

Case study: walking into a room with a phone camera and building a mental 3D model — glance, match corners, guess where you stood — then the same steps as tokens, alternating attention, and heads.

ACT: Action Chunking with Transformers

Imitation learning · RSS 2023 · arXiv 2304.13705

Case study: opening a condiment cup the way a person does it — commit to a short motor phrase, pick one style, keep blending the plan — then chunking, CVAE, temporal ensembling.

STLight

Spatio-temporal prediction · arXiv 2411.10198 · November 2024

Case study: watching a bouncing ball and predicting the next second without narrating every past frame — then joint space–time patches and a convolutional mixer instead of an RNN.

Reading list

Not full guides (yet). Each is a spoke on one of the knowledge graphs above. Open the arXiv if the node is load-bearing for what you are trying to build.

Flow Matching · Lipman et al.

Generative modeling · arXiv 2210.02747 · SmolVLA expert

Velocity fields instead of scores. Why SmolVLA’s action expert can be 0.75d and still sample inside a control cycle.

DUSt3R

3D · arXiv 2312.14132 · VGGT ancestor

Two views → point maps, then global alignment. Read this to see the post-process VGGT deleted.

DINOv2

Self-supervised ViT · arXiv 2304.07193 · VGGT tokens

The patch encoder VGGT barely modifies. Alternating attention sits on top of this.

Diffusion Policy · Chi et al.

Imitation · arXiv 2303.04137 · ACT cousin

Same chunking instinct, diffusion instead of CVAE. LeRobot ships both next to ACT.

PredRNN

STL · arXiv 2103.09504 · STLight baseline

Zigzag spatiotemporal LSTM. The unroll whose FLOPs STLight claims 2% of on KTH.

OpenVLA

VLA · arXiv 2406.09246 · SmolVLA contrast

7B discrete action tokens. The heavy open VLA SmolVLA is sized against.
