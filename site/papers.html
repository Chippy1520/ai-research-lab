SmolVLA + LeRobot

Compact VLA (~450M params) trained on community data. Key ideas: lightweight VLM + flow matching action expert + asynchronous inference. Strong real-world results on affordable robots. All content inside this repo.

← Back

VGGT

Feed-forward transformer for camera poses, depth, point maps and tracks from 1 to hundreds of views. CVPR 2025 Best Paper. Fast inference without optimization pipelines. Content self-contained in AI Research Lab.

← Back

ACT

Action Chunking with Transformers. Predicts sequences of actions to reduce compounding errors. Foundational for many modern imitation learning policies in LeRobot and similar frameworks. Self-contained guide.

STLight

Efficiency-focused techniques for lightweight models in CV and robotics. Important for practical deployment. All material maintained inside this repository.
