# STLight

**STLight Paper Guide** (Efficiency & Lightweight Models in CV / Embodied AI)

## Overview

STLight represents work on creating lighter, more efficient models that retain strong performance for vision or robotics tasks. These approaches are critical for deployment on resource-constrained platforms (edge devices, affordable robots).

## Core Themes Usually Covered

- Model compression, pruning, or architectural redesign for efficiency.
- Maintaining accuracy while reducing parameters, FLOPs, or latency.
- Trade-offs between model size and real-world usability.
- Often includes careful ablations on where efficiency gains come from.

## Why These Papers Matter in the Curriculum

Efficiency papers sit at the intersection of Computer Vision and Embodied AI & RL Robotics. They show how to take powerful but heavy models and make them practical for real robots with limited compute.

## Suggested Reading Route

1. Problem statement: Why do we need smaller models?
2. Proposed efficient architecture or training method.
3. Key ablations and comparisons to baselines.
4. Real-world or edge deployment results.
5. Limitations and when the approach may not apply.
