## Brief description

Video foundation models learn temporal representations or generative dynamics from large video corpora; world-centric perception asks whether those features preserve persistent objects, geometry, motion, and cause beyond short frame correlations.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- Video supervision provides motion and temporal continuity without manual labels.
- Masked prediction, contrastive objectives, and latent prediction preserve different aspects of time.
- A world-centric representation should maintain identity, 3D structure, and dynamics through occlusion and viewpoint change.
- Short-clip accuracy can hide weak long-term memory, physical inconsistency, and dependence on appearance shortcuts.
