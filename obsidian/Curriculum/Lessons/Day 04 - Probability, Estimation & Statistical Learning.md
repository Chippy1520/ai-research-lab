## Brief description

Statistical learning uses finite, noisy samples to estimate patterns that generalize beyond the observed data, separating uncertainty in the data from uncertainty introduced by the model and estimator.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- A parameter, estimator, estimate, and predictive distribution are different objects.
- Bias and variance describe systematic error and sampling sensitivity; neither alone determines generalization.
- Likelihood expresses how a model explains data; priors and regularization inject assumptions.
- Evaluation requires held-out data and a metric aligned with the decision that will use the prediction.
