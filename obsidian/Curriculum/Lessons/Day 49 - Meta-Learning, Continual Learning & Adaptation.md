## Brief description

Meta-learning adapts from a small amount of task data, while continual learning preserves useful knowledge across a sequence of changing tasks; both study how learning itself should change with experience.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- Gradient-based meta-learning optimizes an initialization or update rule for rapid task adaptation.
- Metric and memory-based methods adapt by comparison or retrieval rather than many parameter updates.
- Continual learning must balance plasticity on new data with stability of old capabilities.
- Task boundaries, replay access, evaluation order, and distribution drift determine whether an adaptation claim is meaningful.
