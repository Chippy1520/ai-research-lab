## Brief description

Causal representation learning seeks latent variables and mechanisms that remain meaningful across interventions and environments; invariance supplies a test for distinguishing stable causes from predictive shortcuts.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- Correlation predicts within observed regimes; causal structure predicts selected interventions and shifts.
- An invariant mechanism retains the same conditional relationship across suitable environments.
- Latent causal variables are generally not identifiable without assumptions, interventions, or multiple environments.
- Causal language does not make a representation causal; specify the graph, intervention, and falsifiable invariance claim.
