## Brief description

Interpretability studies what information a model represents and how computation produces behavior; mechanistic analysis traces causal components, while alignment asks whether those mechanisms reliably support intended objectives.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- Attribution is a hypothesis about influence, not automatically a causal explanation.
- Mechanistic work needs interventions such as ablation, activation patching, or controlled feature steering.
- Distributed and superposed representations make neuron-level stories incomplete.
- An interpretable mechanism is useful for alignment only if it predicts failures and supports reliable monitoring or control.
