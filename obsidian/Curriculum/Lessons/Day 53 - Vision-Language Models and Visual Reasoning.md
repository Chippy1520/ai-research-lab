## Brief description

Vision-language models align visual and textual representations or generate language from images; visual reasoning requires grounding intermediate claims in image evidence rather than relying on linguistic priors.

## Recall in 30 seconds

- Contrastive models support retrieval and zero-shot classification; generative models condition a language decoder on visual tokens.
- Resolution, region representation, connector architecture, and instruction data control what visual detail reaches the model.
- Correct language can be produced from dataset priors without inspecting the image.
- Evaluate grounding, counting, spatial relations, OCR, calibration, and adversarial counterexamples separately.
